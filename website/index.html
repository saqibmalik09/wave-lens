Auto-adjust, brightness, contrast, saturation, warm/cool, B&W, vintage, sepia, film looks, and glow — all in one GPU pass.

Face-anchored 2D

Cat ears, crowns, sunglasses, makeup, face paint, festive stickers — anchored to face landmarks with on-demand asset packs.

Background change

Real-time blur, solid color, or image replacement using lightweight segmentation — tuned for rural networks.

Simple licensing

One client_id + client_secret per app. No per-host keys. Enable or disable filters from Studio dashboard.

How Wave Lens works

  1. 1

    We activate your app

    You get a tenant account on studio.wavelens.online with a client_id bound to your app bundle ID.

  2. 2

    Integrate the SDK

    Add the Wave Lens library, call WaveLens.init(), and drop in WaveLensView for the filtered camera preview.

  3. 3

    Hosts go live with filters

    Filter tray shows only what you enabled. Assets download once and cache forever — fully offline after first use.
