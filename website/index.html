Three steps to go live

  1. Add the SDK — one Gradle line + the wavelens AAR.
  2. Initialize once — WaveLens.init(clientId, clientSecret) at app startup.
  3. Show the camera — add WaveLensView where hosts open the filter tray.

Android 7+ (API 24) · Android 10–17 supported · OpenGL ES 3.0 · armeabi-v7a & arm64-v8a
