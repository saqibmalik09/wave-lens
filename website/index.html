Three steps to go live

You do not need a rewrite. If your app already opens a camera, Wave Lens plugs in beside it.

  1. 1

    Add the SDK

    One Gradle dependency and the Wave Lens AAR. Works with Kotlin, Java, and Jetpack Compose today.

  2. 2

    Sign in once at startup

    Call WaveLens.init(clientId, clientSecret) with the keys from Studio. That is your license check.

  3. 3

    Show the filter tray

    Place WaveLensView where your host opens the camera. They swipe filters like Snapchat — you stay in control from Studio.
